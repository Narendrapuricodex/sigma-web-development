# video 8. (elements type)

""""Block Element"""

Element,Description/Use
<div>,Ye ek generic container hai jo dusre elements ko group karne ke kaam aata hai.
<h1> - <h6>,Headings ke liye use hote hain (<h1> sabse bada aur <h6> sabse chhota).
<p>,Paragraphs likhne ke liye use hota hai.
<ul> / <ol> / <li>,Lists (bulleted ya numbered) banane ke liye.
<header> / <footer>,Page ke top ya bottom section ko define karne ke liye.
<section> / <article>,Content ko alag-alag hisson mein divide karne ke liye.

Inline Elements:

Element,Description/Use
<span>,Text ke kisi specific hisse ko style karne ya group karne ke liye container.
<a>,Hyperlinks banane ke liye (ek page se dusre page par jane ke liye).
<img>,Page par images dikhane ke liye.
<strong> / <b>,Text ko bold (important) dikhane ke liye.
<em> / <i>,Text ko italicize karne ke liye.
<button>,Clickable button banane ke liye.
